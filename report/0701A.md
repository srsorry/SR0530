# SR0701A 机器学习方法：10-fold 交叉验证与双眼数据处理

## 中文 Methods

### 1. 数据划分原则：以受试者（Subject）为单位

本研究纳入 **71 只眼** 来自 **46 名受试者**。由于部分受试者同时包含右眼（OD）和左眼（OS），双眼在解剖结构、屈光状态及疾病特征上高度相关，若将双眼作为独立样本进行交叉验证，会导致训练集与测试集之间的信息泄漏，从而高估模型性能并违反统计独立性假设。

因此，所有机器学习模型均采用 **受试者级别（subject-level）的交叉验证**：以受试者唯一标识符 `Subject_ID` 作为分组变量，确保 **同一受试者的双眼始终被划分到同一个 fold 中**，不会同时出现在训练集和测试集中。

### 2. 10-fold GroupKFold 交叉验证

采用 **10-fold GroupKFold** 策略，具体流程如下：

1. **分组**：按 `Subject_ID` 将 71 只眼划分为 46 个独立组。
2. **分层**：根据近视状态（`Myopia`，以等效球镜 ≤ -0.5 D 为界）对受试者进行分层，确保每个 fold 中近视与非近视受试者的比例与总体一致。
3. **划分**：将 46 个受试者组循环分配到 10 个 fold 中，每个 fold 包含互不相交的受试者集合。
4. **训练与验证**：在第 `k` 折中，使用其余 9 折的受试者数据训练模型，并在第 `k` 折的受试者数据上进行验证。重复 10 次，确保每只眼均被验证一次。
5. **指标汇总**：计算每折的 Test R²、RMSE、MAPE 及 Gap（Train R² − Test R²），最终报告 10 折的均值与标准差。

由于分组变量为受试者而非单眼，模型在测试阶段面对的是 **从未见过的受试者**，从而真实反映模型对新个体的泛化能力。

### 3. 超参数调优

对每个模型（Lasso、Ridge、ElasticNet、Robust Linear Regression、SVM、Random Forest、XGBoost、Neural Network）进行随机搜索调优（30 组参数 / 模型）。调优过程中同样采用上述 **10-fold GroupKFold by Subject** 策略，以避免超参数选择阶段的数据泄漏。最终选择 **10-fold 平均 Test R² 最高** 的参数组合作为该模型的最佳配置。

### 4. 特征与目标变量

- **目标变量**：Angular cone density（cones/deg²），即光感受器角密度。
- **特征变量**：根据方案不同，包括眼轴长度（AL）、角膜曲率（K）、前房深度（ACD）、等效球镜（SER）、年龄（Age）、性别（Gender）及 AL/K ratio。
- **缺失值处理**：对连续变量采用中位数填充。
- **标准化**：线性模型无需标准化；Neural Network 在每次 fold 内对特征和目标变量分别进行标准化，并在预测时反变换回原始尺度。

### 5. 模型评估指标

- **R²**：决定系数，反映模型解释方差的比例。
- **RMSE**：均方根误差，单位与目标变量一致（cones/deg²）。
- **RMSE_std**：标准化 RMSE，即 RMSE / 目标变量标准差，便于跨研究比较。
- **MAPE**：平均绝对百分比误差。
- **Gap**：Train R² − Test R²，用于评估过拟合程度。

### 6. 关键说明：为什么不能用随机 KFold？

若采用普通随机 KFold，同一受试者的左眼可能在训练集、右眼在测试集。由于双眼共享大量个体特征，模型可以通过“记忆”该受试者的训练眼来预测测试眼，导致 R² 被人为抬高、RMSE 被人为压低。GroupKFold by Subject 彻底避免了这一问题，是眼科影像机器学习研究中的标准做法。

## English Methods

### 1. Data Splitting Principle: Subject-Level Partitioning

This study included **71 eyes** from **46 subjects**. Because some subjects contributed both the right eye (OD) and the left eye (OS), and because the two eyes of the same individual are highly correlated in anatomy, refractive status, and disease characteristics, treating eyes as independent samples during cross-validation would cause information leakage between the training and test sets. This would artificially inflate model performance and violate the assumption of statistical independence.

Therefore, all machine-learning models were evaluated using **subject-level cross-validation**: the unique `Subject_ID` was used as the grouping variable to ensure that **both eyes of the same subject were always assigned to the same fold** and never appeared simultaneously in the training and test sets.

### 2. 10-fold GroupKFold Cross-Validation

A **10-fold GroupKFold** strategy was adopted, with the following procedure:

1. **Grouping**: The 71 eyes were grouped into 46 independent groups according to `Subject_ID`.
2. **Stratification**: Subjects were stratified by myopia status (`Myopia`, defined as spherical equivalent refraction ≤ -0.5 D) to preserve the overall proportion of myopic versus non-myopic subjects in each fold.
3. **Splitting**: The 46 subject groups were cyclically assigned to 10 folds, with each fold containing a disjoint set of subjects.
4. **Training and Validation**: In the `k`-th fold, the model was trained on data from the other 9 folds and validated on the `k`-th fold. This was repeated 10 times so that every eye was validated exactly once.
5. **Metric Aggregation**: Test R², RMSE, MAPE, and Gap (Train R² − Test R²) were computed for each fold, and the means and standard deviations across the 10 folds were reported.

Because the grouping variable was the subject rather than the individual eye, the model was evaluated on **previously unseen subjects**, thereby reflecting the true generalization ability to new individuals.

### 3. Hyperparameter Tuning

For each model (Lasso, Ridge, ElasticNet, Robust Linear Regression, SVM, Random Forest, XGBoost, Neural Network), random-search hyperparameter tuning was performed (30 parameter sets per model). The same **10-fold GroupKFold by Subject** strategy was used during tuning to avoid data leakage in the hyperparameter selection stage. The parameter combination with the **highest mean 10-fold Test R²** was selected as the final configuration for each model.

### 4. Features and Target Variable

- **Target variable**: Angular cone density (cones/deg²).
- **Feature variables**: Depending on the feature set, predictors included axial length (AL), corneal curvature (K), anterior chamber depth (ACD), spherical equivalent refraction (SER), age, gender, and the AL/K ratio.
- **Missing-value imputation**: Continuous variables were imputed with the median.
- **Standardization**: Linear models did not require standardization. For the Neural Network, features and the target were standardized within each training fold and inverse-transformed back to the original scale for prediction.

### 5. Model Evaluation Metrics

- **R²**: Coefficient of determination, indicating the proportion of variance explained.
- **RMSE**: Root mean squared error, in the same unit as the target variable (cones/deg²).
- **RMSE_std**: Standardized RMSE, defined as RMSE divided by the standard deviation of the target variable, to facilitate cross-study comparison.
- **MAPE**: Mean absolute percentage error.
- **Gap**: Train R² − Test R², used to assess overfitting.

### 6. Why Random KFold Is Not Appropriate

With ordinary random KFold, the left eye of a subject might end up in the training set while the right eye is placed in the test set. Because the two eyes share many subject-specific characteristics, the model could effectively "memorize" the training eye and predict the test eye, leading to spuriously high R² and artificially low RMSE. GroupKFold by Subject eliminates this problem completely and is the standard practice in ophthalmic imaging machine-learning research.
